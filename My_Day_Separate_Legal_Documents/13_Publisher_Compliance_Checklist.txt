MY DAY — PUBLISHER LEGAL AND COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
Internal checklist — not a user-facing policy

Before release:
[ ] Replace every placeholder in the legal documents.
[ ] Confirm the legal publisher/owner and who is authorised to publish.
[ ] Have a parent/guardian and an Indian lawyer review the publishing and licensing arrangements.
[ ] Check ownership or permission for all code, fonts, icons, images, sounds, and other assets.
[ ] Inventory all bundled Python packages and include their licence notices.
[ ] Verify the actual local database/configuration paths.
[ ] Verify whether the app sends any network requests in the shipped build.
[ ] Ensure the Privacy Policy matches actual code and data flows.
[ ] Ensure the README explains installation, uninstallation, data location, and backup.
[ ] Keep version numbers and effective dates consistent.

Before enabling online features:
[ ] Map every data flow, including telemetry and crash reporting.
[ ] Identify each service provider and its role.
[ ] Decide data retention and deletion periods.
[ ] Implement access controls and security measures before claiming them.
[ ] Add clear user notices and any legally required consent.
[ ] Provide user access, correction, export, and deletion mechanisms where required.
[ ] Review applicable Indian privacy, consumer, tax, cybersecurity, and intellectual-property requirements.
[ ] Update the Privacy Policy, Terms, and Online Services Addendum.
[ ] Test account closure and data deletion.
[ ] Review the live payment and cancellation journey if payments are introduced.

Legal note:
These drafts are not legal advice or a compliance certificate. Publishing policies alone does not make an app compliant. Check laws and commencement dates in force on the actual release date.
